# The design is compressed measurement

Lineage (Michael, 2026-08-28): llm-repl was DESIGNED after ~350 sessions of
verbum (tensor-side study of the native calculus) and 60+ of anima. The
architecture doc's formal-shape clauses are not post-hoc analogies — each
traces to a measured substrate property: fork-cheap/splice-absent ← cache
physics (prefix-pure fork EXACT, chunk-shift APPROXIMATE) · messages[] ≡
truth ⊕ byte-identical ← the machine is HYPERINTENSIONAL (individuates by
tape FORM) · compact!'s band/receipts/:original ← the tape has no author
column (undeclared edits absorbed invisibly) · trampoline!/ab! ← vary one
thing off a fixed KV point, gate on δ (the memetic-GA move) · :complete-fn
injected-never-stored ← the calculus is the SUBJECT; the instrument must
not absorb it.

Consequences: (1) "ratified" ≡ measurement-backed — do not casually
redesign a clause whose evidence lives in ~/src/verbum or ~/src/anima;
recall across the seam before re-deriving. (2) Hyperintensionality is WHY
byte-exact EDN capture matters for trace-durability: refine-turn is a
re-entry into the same machine state only if the tape form is exact — the
proposal's replay claim rests on this, not on serialization convenience.
